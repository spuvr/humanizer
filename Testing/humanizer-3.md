People have been robbing bees for at least 8,000 years. There's a cave painting in Spain of someone halfway up a cliff with one hand in a hive and the other clutching a basket, which tells you roughly how badly we wanted the honey. The Egyptians kept bees in clay tubes and buried jars of honey in their tombs that came out edible 3,000 years later. From there the hives got fancier and the production scaled up, and now California's almond crop physically cannot happen without trucks hauling billions of bees in every February. Which is also why colony collapse stopped being a hobbyist worry and started being an agriculture one.
